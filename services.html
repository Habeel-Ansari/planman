Services

From Requirements to After-Sales Support

We cover every stage of your project: understanding the requirement, designing the solution, supplying the hardware, deploying it and supporting it for years after go-live.

Requirement Analysis & Design

Site surveys, stakeholder workshops, solution architecture, bill of materials and budget planning.

  • GPU, CPU and memory sizing
  • Network topology and fabric design
  • Rack elevation and facility requirements

System & Rack Integration

We build, configure and validate systems at server, rack and cluster level before shipping to site.

  • L10 system, L11 rack and L12 cluster integration
  • Firmware baselining and BIOS tuning
  • Burn-in, NCCL and HPL acceptance testing

Onsite Deployment

Rack and stack, structured cabling, fabric bring-up and OS and software stack installation at your data center.

  • Copper, fibre and optics labelling
  • InfiniBand and Ethernet fabric bring-up
  • Handover documentation

Liquid Cooling Integration

Direct liquid cooling for high-density GPU racks: CDU selection, manifolds, facility water and commissioning.

  • Liquid-to-liquid and liquid-to-air CDUs
  • Rear-door heat exchangers
  • Leak detection and monitoring

Clustering & Platform Setup

Configured, production-ready platforms on top of the hardware we deliver.

  • Slurm, Kubernetes and GPU Operator
  • VMware, Proxmox, Nutanix and Hyper-V HA clusters
  • NVIDIA AI Enterprise and vGPU licensing

Hosting & Colocation

Run your infrastructure in a UAE data center with our team handling the physical layer.

  • Dedicated and bare-metal GPU servers
  • Rack space, power and remote hands
  • Private GPU cloud environments

Managed Services

Ongoing operation of your environment with monitoring, patching and lifecycle management.

  • 24/7 health and performance monitoring
  • Firmware and driver lifecycle
  • Capacity planning and expansion

After-Sales Support

Local support after handover: warranty claims, vendor RMA, maintenance and upgrades to keep downtime to a minimum.

  • Local spares stocking options
  • Vendor warranty and RMA handling
  • Onsite break-fix

Enterprise Hardware Supply

Complete servers, storage, GPUs, networking and workstations from all premium brands, for end customers, resellers and integrators.

  • Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Cisco, NVIDIA and more
  • Project and bulk pricing
  • Export and regional logistics
Built for Density

Liquid-Cooled, Rack-Ready, Tested

Modern GPU racks draw more power than air alone can cool. We plan direct liquid cooling and power delivery with your facility team, then integrate and test every rack before it ships, so installation day is about plugging in, not troubleshooting.

  • CDU sizing, manifolds and facility water loops
  • Rack-level power, cabling and firmware baselines
  • Burn-in and performance validation before shipping
