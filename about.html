About Plan Man

The Engine for Your Digital Success

Who We Are

Solution Provider. Hardware Supplier. Long-Term Partner.

Plan Man helps organisations across the UAE plan, build and run their IT infrastructure. We focus on hospitality, education, manufacturing and corporate enterprise, supplying complete servers, storage, GPUs and networking from all premium brands.

Our role goes beyond selling hardware. We gather your requirements, design the right solution, supply and deploy it, and stay with you through after-sales support, whether it's a new hotel, a university AI lab or a factory server room.
